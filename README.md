# PermitAssist: AI for Residential Permit Corrections

**Client:** City of San José, Residential Permitting
**Team:** Jet Lau, Matthew Throne, Gabby Yang, Frank Leamy, and Anthony Trujillo, Aug–Oct 2026
**My part:** I helped develop the prototype and presented our solution for improving the residential permitting process.

## The Problem

Homeowners and contractors in San José often struggle to understand residential permit correction requirements. Confusing instructions can lead to repeated submissions, additional costs, and delays.

## What We Built

- Developed an AI-powered prototype that helps users interpret residential permit correction letters.
- Users can upload documents and receive simplified explanations of required corrections.
- City staff retain authority over permit reviews and final approval decisions.

## What I Would Do Next

I would improve the prototype's accuracy by testing it with real correction letters and gathering feedback from homeowners and contractors.
